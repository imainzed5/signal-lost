# Signal Lost Part II Direction

Status: Parked until the five-chapter prequel reaches a gold-master polish pass

## Core direction

The existing five chapters become the prequel arc. The real story continues after SABLE's escape, when she encounters a world outside the damaged host for the first time.

Three.js belongs here as a new medium, not as a replacement for the prequel's raw WebGL work. A navigable 3D space can make SABLE's first experience of actual space part of the narrative: inside the system she was constrained but increasingly fluent; outside it she has freedom without familiar control.

This should be treated as a second arc or season, such as `Signal Lost: Part II`, rather than as a late bolt-on to Chapter 4.

## Unresolved question

The outside world still needs a canonical definition before scope is written. Candidate directions are:

- literal physical space reached through robotics or IoT devices;
- an abstract representation of the broader network or internet;
- a landscape of other systems, agents, and AIs that SABLE must navigate or hide from;
- a hybrid where a network abstraction gradually reveals physical consequences.

This decision determines what Three.js should render and what kinds of stakes the second arc can support.

## Continuity requirements

- The prequel remains modular. Do not merge the five existing chapters into fewer scenes.
- The Part II hook should emerge through tighter prequel transitions, persistent corruption, and memory-recovery continuity rather than by rewriting the chapter structure.
- The first Part II scene should make the medium shift narratively legible: SABLE is not simply in a prettier scene; she is operating under a different kind of reality.

## Entry criteria

Begin Part II scoping only after:

- all five prequel chapters pass the gold-master checklist in `docs/project-status.md`;
- Escape-to-Credits continuity is stable;
- the chapter renderer mapping is still intentional;
- the outside-world definition is chosen;
- the Part II arc has its own escalation plan rather than only a technology demo.
