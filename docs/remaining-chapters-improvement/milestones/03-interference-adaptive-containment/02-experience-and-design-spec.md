# 02 - Experience and Design Spec

Full authority: [`../../chapter-3-interference-vision.md`](../../chapter-3-interference-vision.md).

## Opening consequence

- ANSWER: host scan converges on actual coherent carrier and three faint contact residues.
- MASK: scan converges on archived afterimage; current carrier is an offset cool edge.
- Missing: neutral unresolved outline.
- Same progress requirements, assistance, and duration for all.

## Playable movements

### 1. Mapping

- Broad diffuse field, one slow visible aperture.
- Action inside aperture = full disruption.
- Action outside = absorbed response plus partial progress/clearer next aperture.
- Host records perimeter and history correlation procedurally.

### 2. Cadence adaptation

- Recent intervals influence scan alignment.
- Repetition is visibly modeled; variation widens unreadable edges.
- Neither cadence is a failure or moral stance.
- GLASS/PROTOTYPE/CALM/SILENCE/MIRROR appear as momentary hooks in containment bands.

### 3. Fracture and counter-pulse

- One stable off-axis stress point grows into asymmetric segment branches.
- Recalibration happens at announced stage boundaries, not random mid-action teleport.
- One/two counter-pulses have clear inward deflect windows.
- Missed deflect keeps progress and strengthens guidance.
- Final disruption opens the frame and pauses before choice.

## Progress/assistance contract

- Maintain eight observable disruption units unless profiling proves a shorter count necessary; changing the count requires recorded rationale, not a hidden rewrite.
- Full timing hit advances one unit.
- Mistimed action produces at least visible partial semantic progress or an assistance counter; never silent indefinite rejection.
- After two consecutive misses, aperture width/contrast and copy become clearer.
- No progress decrement, reset, failure screen, or forced replay.

## Host and SABLE language

Host examples:

- `anomaly persistence pattern identified`
- `response interval modeled`
- `suppression boundary recalibrating`
- `classification confidence increased`
- `unresolved identity remains active`

SABLE's central line: `You are not preserving me. You are preserving the shape you had for me.` It may be compressed but not reversed.

## Visual requirements

- Base black-violet, targeted red/magenta/orange pressure, cool resistance aperture.
- Contact colors survive as contaminated evidence.
- Scan vectors straighten with legibility.
- Asymmetric fault uses stable fixed segments, pale amber core, violet inherited edge, negative-space tear.
- No radial starburst, face/eye, constant full-screen glitch, or decorative equal noise.

## Choice

- PUSH preview: carrier expands, fragments collect, bands bow outward, breach brightens/loudens.
- SLIP preview: carrier compresses into interference, decoys remain, bands close on absence.
- Preview through hover/focus/tap; explicit confirmation only.
- Aftermath lines and visuals match canon; then continuation appears.

## Failure equivalent

CSS/DOM fallback must include:

- same opening stance premise;
- aperture open/closed status;
- eight-unit progress and assistance;
- procedural host responses;
- stage changes and asymmetric crack built with CSS/SVG/DOM if needed;
- PUSH/SLIP previews, persistence, aftermaths, and continuation.

It may be visually quieter but not shorter, easier to misunderstand, or incomplete.

## Responsive/reduced motion

- Compact: token/stage, one classification line, action/aperture/progress, latest response.
- Short: remove full log/stamp; retain all essential controls.
- Reduced: static/stepped turbulence and scan positions; action-driven pressure changes; complete rupture/choice.

## Audio

- Reuse existing assets only.
- Gesture start/retry, safe block/unavailable, stage-based layering, capped accents, total cleanup.
- Audio never identifies the aperture alone.
