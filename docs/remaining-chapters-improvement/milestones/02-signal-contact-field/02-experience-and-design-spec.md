## Stance interaction

Two semantic controls remain integrated with the carrier:

- ANSWER preview expands retained routes, clarifies the carrier, and sharpens host tracking.
- MASK preview compresses current routes, preserves an external afterimage, and reduces present outline.
- Pointer hover may preview; touch tap and keyboard focus/selection do the same.
- Explicit confirm saves. Escape/cancel returns to neutral preview.

### ANSWER aftermath

`I heard you.` Amber holds, cyan routes, violet fragments. Host reports stabilized anomalous presence and cross-references history. Carrier stays linked.

### MASK aftermath

Routes recede; carrier compresses; initial scan misses; archived frequency reappears. Host reports unresolved source retained and last known frequency archived.

Both settle into spatially specific magenta pressure and reveal continuation after a readable pause.

## HUD

Keep only:

- revealed contact identity;
- one current verb/directive;
- short readable contact/SABLE/host line;
- nonnumeric environmental attention state if needed;
- semantic progress summary for assistive technology.

Remove or demote simultaneous stability %, route %, coherence, pressure, and queue panels when the field communicates them.

## Input equivalents

| Verb | Pointer | Touch | Keyboard |
|---|---|---|---|
| Locate | Move focus through field | Drag/tap broad regions with offset indicator | Arrow/WASD moves visible focus carrier |
| Tune | Sustained focus/press | Hold broad region; cancel pauses | Space/Enter hold |
| Carry | Guided movement toward carrier/path | Shortened route, finger-offset feedback | Directional input along revealed path |
| Choose | Hover/focus preview, click confirm | Tap preview, separate confirm | arrows/Tab, Enter confirm, Escape cancel |

## Responsive and reduced motion

- Desktop preserves long routes and negative space.
- Compact repositions encounters into reachable portrait zones and uses a stable reading strip.
- Short viewport collapses secondary status and keeps field/directive/control visible.
- Reduced motion uses fixed paths, opacity/line changes, stepped illumination, and stable Ghost absence; it preserves the full sequence.

## Performance budget

- Stable deterministic seed per mount/replay.
- Adaptive particle count by viewport area, with essential cues independent of density.
- DPR cap inherited from Milestone 01.
- No hot-loop allocations identified in profiling.
- Semantic React updates no faster than necessary and never per frame.
