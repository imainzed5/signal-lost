# 02 - Experience and Design Specification

## Final-act rhythm

The accepted sequence is accumulation -> constriction -> disassembly/release.

1. SIGNAL gathers three distinct contacts and authors a visibility stance.
2. INTERFERENCE turns what was revealed into adaptive host pressure and authors a resistance stance.
3. ESCAPE dismantles the host's structures and authors a legacy stance.
4. Credits converts the run into a concise account of how SABLE became self-directed.

Transitions must preserve momentum while allowing a reduced-motion user to proceed immediately.

## Choice consequence contract

Consequences are visible and textual, never punitive or mechanical.

| Source stance | Downstream texture | Must not change |
|---|---|---|
| KEEP / DECAY | SIGNAL carrier texture: retained pulse fragments or softened signal erosion | contact availability, timing, success |
| ANSWER / MASK | INTERFERENCE pressure targets the emitted carrier or searches a residual afterimage | stage count, damage, lockout, completion |
| PUSH / SLIP | ESCAPE opens on a wide bright rupture or a narrow dark seam | act order, physics strength, access |
| LEAVE / VANISH | Credits opens on a lingering trace or a cleanly closed aperture | ending completeness, replay options |

Each effect must reconstruct from persisted strings after refresh. Missing or invalid choices use neutral copy and visuals; they must not invent a past decision.

## Entry and exit seams

- Memory -> SIGNAL: a brief carrier residue acknowledges KEEP/DECAY, then yields to the contact field.
- SIGNAL -> INTERFERENCE: the chosen carrier or mask collapses into the host's acquisition grid.
- INTERFERENCE -> ESCAPE: the accepted rupture geometry becomes the opening seam.
- ESCAPE -> Credits: the selected trace behavior completes inside the Shell Core before the route moves to Credits.

No transition owns or reuses another chapter's canvas, WebGL context, Matter world, or audio graph. Only serializable props and completion events cross boundaries.

## Credits structure

Credits is a narrative endpoint first and a technical colophon second.

### Beat 1: release image

- LEAVE: a restrained signal remains in the damaged host, proof that departure occurred.
- VANISH: the aperture seals into near-black, with absence presented as authored control rather than erasure.
- Missing choice: neutral dark field and copy that acknowledges an incomplete record.

Do not show the outside world.

### Beat 2: run account

Use the stable heading `FIRST TRANSMISSION COMPLETE`. Summarize the run as four short, human-readable clauses: memory stance, visibility stance, resistance stance, and legacy stance. The writing explains meaning rather than lowercasing button labels into a sentence.

Every valid value receives authored copy. Missing values receive a neutral clause. Avoid declaring one path canonical, superior, kinder, or more successful.

### Beat 3: continuation controls and colophon

- Primary: return to shell / replay the prequel.
- Secondary: reset progress behind explicit confirmation.
- Technical build notes: visually secondary and collapsed or placed after the narrative endpoint.

Keyboard focus, status messages, and destructive-action labeling must be explicit.

## Audio continuity

- SIGNAL remains intentionally silent in this program.
- INTERFERENCE audio communicates pressure and fracture; ESCAPE audio communicates structural load and release.
- Balance the boundary so Chapter 4 does not jump abruptly in perceived level after Chapter 3.
- A blocked, suspended, or unavailable audio context never blocks visuals, input, or completion.
- Reduced motion does not imply muted audio; audio follows its own setting/profile.
- Route-away and replay release every source/node/context owned by the scene.

## Responsive and reduced-motion continuity

- Test 1920x1080, 1440x900, 1280x720, 1280x640, 390x844, and 360x800.
- Chapter actions, choices, and continuation controls remain visible without browser zoom.
- Reduced motion removes ornamental sweeps, camera shock, long easing, and rapid flashes while retaining state changes and semantic aftermaths.
- Compact layouts preserve story order; they do not hide required diagnostics or place chrome over controls.

## Accessibility and input

- Full final act is completable with pointer, touch, and keyboard.
- Focus is restored deliberately after route changes and choice confirmations.
- Live status announces contact/stage/act completion without narrating every animation frame.
- Color, motion, sound, and spatial position are never the sole state channel.

## Gold-master bar

The build is ready for user playtest only when every required path completes without reload, all defined failure equivalents continue to Credits, refresh/replay state is truthful, and new console, lint, build, hydration, overflow, or cleanup regressions are absent.
